## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Habib**, a Computer Science and Engineering student at **United International University (UIU)**, Bangladesh.

I'm interested in software development, problem-solving, and building practical applications that address real-world needs. I enjoy learning how software works, experimenting with different technologies, and turning ideas into working products.

I'm also involved in building digital products and software solutions through **[Eidhl](https://eidhl.com)**.

- 🎓 Studying Computer Science & Engineering at UIU

- 🚀 Building practical projects and applications
- 🗄️ Learning Data Structures, Algorithms, and Database Systems
- 🤖 Exploring Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
- 📚 Interested in research and continuous learning
